# Chestnut Hill Village Association

The homeowners on Chestnut Road, Village Lane, and Orchard Way. Dues are **$10/year**, payable at the annual meeting.

**Current president:** Marcy (since 2026)

## Dates

| What | When |
|---|---|
| Annual in-person meeting | first Tuesday in March, 7pm, at the Chestnut Road clubhouse |
| Annual spring cleanup | second Saturday in April, 9am — rain date the following Saturday |

Contractor recommendations and the cleanup work list are agreed at the annual meeting, so both sections below are reviewed there each March.
